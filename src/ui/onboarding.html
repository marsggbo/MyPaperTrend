📈 Welcome to MyPaperTrend

Your Google Scholar citations, tracked over time — right on your profile.

1️⃣
Open a Google Scholar profile & flip “Track”
Go to any Scholar profile (yours or anyone you follow) and turn on the Track switch in the panel. Nothing is recorded until you do — so different people’s data never mix.
2️⃣
It reads the page directly — no scraping API, no login
MyPaperTrend reads the numbers already on your screen (including Scholar’s own citation total, correct even when paginated). Nothing is scraped from a server, so Google never blocks it.
3️⃣
Set it and forget it
Turn on daily auto-update in Settings and pick a time — it refreshes every tracked scholar automatically (reading all their papers) whenever your browser is open. Or click “Capture all pages” any time.
🔒 Your data stays on your device. History is stored locally in your browser — no account required. You can export it any time, or optionally connect Google Drive / a GitHub repo in Advanced settings.
